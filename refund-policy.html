Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

We keep this simple: no long-term contracts, cancel any month. This policy explains exactly what happens when you cancel and when refunds apply. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

How to cancel

Send us a message by email at info@flooringwebpros.com or on WhatsApp at +1 (860) 255-1879 saying you'd like to cancel. To avoid being charged for the next month, please let us know before your next billing date. We'll confirm your cancellation and the date your services end.

What happens when you cancel

  • Your services continue until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.
  • Your website goes offline at the end of that period, when we disconnect your domain from our hosting. The website design and code remain the property of Flooring WebPros.
  • Your domain stays yours. You can point it to any other website or provider.
  • Your content stays yours. On request before your services end, we'll send you copies of the photos and logo you provided to us.
  • Accounts we managed, such as your Google Business Profile, Facebook and Instagram, belong to you. We'll remove our access and, if needed, help make sure you have full control.

Refunds

Plans are billed monthly in advance. Because work and hosting start as soon as a billing period begins, monthly fees are non-refundable, and we don't offer partial refunds for unused days in a billing period.

We will always refund:

  • Duplicate charges or billing errors on our side.
  • Any charge made after you cancelled before your billing date.

If you're unhappy with our work, please tell us. We'd much rather fix the problem than lose you.

Changing plans

You can move up or down between Launch, Build and Scale at any time. The new price applies from your next billing date, and there's no refund for the difference in the current period.

Coming back

If you'd like to return after cancelling, just get in touch. Depending on how long you've been away, we may be able to restore your previous website.
