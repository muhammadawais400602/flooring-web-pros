## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an architectural, warm Scandinavian and Japandi sensibility tailored for an organic minimalist interior brand. It bridges craftsmanship and digital precision, evoking calmness, tactile permanence, and material honesty.

The visual style merges warm minimalism with an editorial layout aesthetic:
- **Atmospheric Space:** Broad margins, deliberate pauses, and uncluttered viewports that let timber textures and spatial proportions breathe.
- **Architectural Structure:** Fine tonal boundaries and baseline alignment inspired by architectural drafting and Scandinavian joinery.
- **Material Authenticity:** Surfaces rely on layered oat and linen tones rather than stark synthetic whites, producing a grounded, contemplative user experience.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from raw wood species, stone, and vegetal dyes, establishing a quiet and tactile atmosphere without high-contrast digital glare.

- **Primary (`#8C6A48` - Warm Umber / Natural Oak):** Anchors focal interactive elements, primary links, and key brand highlights. Reflects cured white oak.
- **Secondary (`#2B241D` - Deep Espresso / Rich Bark):** Used for primary typography, refined strokes, and grounding accents. Delivers high contrast while maintaining an organic brown undertone rather than pure black.
- **Tertiary (`#6E7758` - Olive Sage):** Serves as an earthy botanical accent for sustainability cues, certifications, badges, and secondary affirmative states.
- **Neutral Surface (`#EDE8DF` - Pale Sand):** Powers secondary cards, subtle dividers, pill containers, and input surface backgrounds.
- **Canvas Base (`#F8F6F0` - Warm Oat):** The overarching light viewport background, avoiding sterile hex whites in favor of an organic paper-and-linen warmth.

## Typography

The typography pairs an editorial high-contrast transitional serif (`Playfair Display`) with a balanced, humanist sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for architectural chapter titles, narrative headers, and timber collection names. The letterforms convey craftsmanship and timeless luxury. Italics may be used selectively for material annotations or quiet emphasis.
- **Body & Captions (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures clear legibility for technical architectural specifications, plank dimensions, installation guides, and narrative descriptions.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tracked letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to mimic architectural blueprint labels and gallery catalog indexes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined 12-column editorial grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width container of 1440px with `3rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content alternates between full-bleed imagery and inset editorial columns with asymmetric margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Vertical stack rhythm relies on `space-xl` between sections to maintain airiness without excessive scrolling friction.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-2xl`) separate curated room-scene sections, creating the measured pacing of a design monograph.

## Elevation & Depth

In line with Japandi and Scandinavian minimalism, depth is expressed through subtle tonal transitions rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas rests on `#F8F6F0` (Warm Oat). Elevating a card or interactive surface is achieved by sitting on `#EDE8DF` (Pale Sand) or pure `#FFFFFF` with a sheer `1px` border tinted to `rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.08)`.
- **Natural Ambient Shading:** When an interactive element demands detachment (such as floating sample trays or modal overlays), use a soft, warm-tinted shadow:
  - `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.03)`
- **Tactile Outlines:** Ghost borders (`1px solid rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.12)`) define clean product spec boundaries without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, low-radius curvature (`roundedness: 1`), evoking planed timber edges, smoothed oak veneers, and architectural joinery rather than bulbous digital interfaces.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Form fields, action buttons, swatch selectors, and input boxes.
- **Medium Radius (0.5rem / 8px - `rounded-lg`):** Spec cards, material swatches, and timber sample tiles.
- **Large Radius (0.75rem / 12px - `rounded-xl`):** Flyout drawers, modal windows, and hero media containers.
- **Plank Proportion Metaphor:** Ratio cards and aspect ratios frequently mirror plank dimensions (e.g., elongated 16:9 or 3:1 banners) to reinforce the flooring medium.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#8C6A48` background with `#F8F6F0` text, subtle `0.25rem` radius, uppercase tracking (`label-md`). Hover transitions smoothly to `#2B241D`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #2B241D`, `#2B241D` text. Hover shifts to `#EDE8DF` surface fill.
- **Tertiary / Text:** Understated underline link with an offset baseline, displaying an arrow glyph `→` for editorial progression.

### Chips & Swatches
- **Spec Filters:** Pill-like containers using `#EDE8DF` background with `#2B241D` text at `label-sm`. Active states flip to `#2B241D` fill with `#F8F6F0` text.
- **Wood Finish Chips:** Square or plank-ratio thumbnails featuring a delicate inset border (`1px solid rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.15)`) with an active state highlighted by a `#8C6A48` ring.

### Cards
- **Product & Moodboard Cards:** Flat surfaces on `#FFFFFF` or `#EDE8DF`, with soft `0.5rem` corners and zero shadow. Image headers maintain raw aspect ratios without artificial rounding along internal seams. Product titles use `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Uncluttered background (`#FFFFFF` or `#EDE8DF`), framed by `1px solid rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.2)`. On focus, the border shifts to `#8C6A48` with no harsh glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square frames (`0.125rem` radius for checkboxes, circular for radios) tinted `#2B241D` when selected, with warm off-white marks.

### Lists & Technical Spec Tables
- Spec tables for engineered hardwood (wear layer, core material, plank width, acoustic rating) use thin hairline rules (`rgba(43, 36, 29, 0.1)`) with labels set in tracked `label-sm` and values in `body-md`.
