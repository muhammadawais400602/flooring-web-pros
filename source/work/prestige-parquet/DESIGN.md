## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sovereign architectural prestige of bespoke European marquetry and parqueterie. Built for private commissions, châteaux conservators, and world-class interior architects, the aesthetic pairs the rigor of classical French cabinetmaking with high-fashion editorial tension.

The design movement blends Haute-Couture Editorial Drama with Architectural Minimalism:
- **Absolute Geometry:** Uncompromising rectilinear precision honoring raw timber joints, mitred angles, and classical symmetry.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Razor-thin hairline frames juxtaposed against grand, dramatic Bodoni display letterforms.
- **Material Restraint:** Obsidian tones, deep mineral greens, and warm antique gilded brass simulate rare patinas, polished walnut, and historic salon atmospheres.
- **Tactile Reserve:** Flat planar layering with zero corner radii; elevation is created through tonal depth and light-catching metallic hairlines rather than diffused digital drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the interior atmosphere of 18th-century salons illuminated by low amber candlelight.

- **Primary (`#C8A355` — Gilded Brass):** Used for focal brand statements, active state accents, precision calipers, key interactive underlines, and gilded borders.
- **Secondary (`#0C241B` — Château Forest):** A profound, blackened royal emerald evoking historic French estate reserves; applied to deep backdrop elevations, hero banner backgrounds, and modal containers.
- **Tertiary (`#F9F6F0` — Champagne Cream):** The primary reading ink and typographic color. Warm, luminous, and legible against deep noir planes without the harsh vibration of pure white.
- **Neutral (`#141715` — Parisian Charcoal Noir):** The default canvas floor. An ultra-deep carbon tint that absorbs ambient light, serving as the velvet ground for metallic and emerald planes.
- **Alabaster White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for high-contrast accents, micro-badges, technical grain schematics, and stark photographic framing.

## Typography

Typography functions as couture architectural framing:

- **Bodoni Moda:** Drives the narrative prestige. Featuring extreme vertical stress, sharp bracketed serifs, and hairline horizontals. Display roles demand ample breathing room; use italics selectively for provenance, timber grades, and bespoke dimensions.
- **Manrope:** Provides crystalline architectural counterpoint. Its clean, structural geometry grounds technical grain descriptions, installation specifications, and curation details without distracting from the woodcraft visuals.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing:** Display headings remain in proper case with slightly tightened tracking. All meta-labels, pattern identifiers, and status indications require uppercase treatment with generous tracking (`0.18em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural grid of classic parquet floorplans (Versailles panel modules, chevron angles, and herringbone alignments):

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column rigid grid desktop layouts with fixed gutters of `2rem` (`32px`) and generous outer margins of `4rem` (`64px`) to preserve white space. Content snaps into column modules of 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12.
- **Reflow Architecture:**
  - *Desktop (≥1200px):* Full 12-column span, large photographic galleries, side-by-side technical schematics.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1199px):* 8-column layout; gutter reduces to `1.5rem`, outer margins to `2.5rem`.
  - *Mobile (≤767px):* 4-column layout; gutters clamp to `1rem` and margins to `1.5rem`. Architectural comparisons stack vertically.
- **Component Padding Rhythm:** Internal containers honor the scale: cards and list modules use `space-lg` (`2rem`) vertical and horizontal padding, creating gallery-grade breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial dropped shadows in favor of atelier craft:

- **Hairline Framing:** Surface separation is achieved entirely via `1px` borders in semi-opaque Gilded Brass (`rgba(200, 163, 85, 0.25)`) or muted slate (`rgba(249, 246, 240, 0.08)`).
- **Tonal Planes:**
  - **Base Canvas (Elevation 0):** Parisian Charcoal Noir (`#141715`).
  - **Panels & Modules (Elevation 1):** Chateau Forest deep tint (`#0C241B`) or raised charcoal (`#191C1A`).
  - **Interactive Popovers & Drawers (Elevation 2):** Layered `#111E18` enclosed in unbroken solid gilded brass hairlines (`#C8A355`).
- **Gilded Luminescence:** Rather than blur shadows, elevated active modals and sample selectors receive a focused edge glow via `inset 0 0 0 1px #C8A355`.

## Shapes

Corner rounding is strictly set to `0` across every viewport, component, and surface.

- **Zero-Radius Mandate:** Every card, button, modal window, badge, image container, and input element has razor-sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Structural Integrity:** Reflects the razor-cut joints of French herringbone and Versailles wood panels. Rounded corners degrade the architectural authority of the atelier.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Sovereign Button:** Razor rectangular shape, solid antique gilded brass background (`#C8A355`), charcoal text (`#141715`), font `Manrope` uppercase, weight 600, letter-spacing `0.18em`. Zero border-radius. On hover, background shifts to Champagne Cream (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Secondary / Atelier Outline:** Transparent background with a `1px` solid gilded brass border (`#C8A355`), text in Champagne Cream. Hover state floods the container with Château Forest (`#0C241B`).
- **Text Action:** Bodoni or Manrope link framed with a permanent, delicate `1px` brass underline offset by `6px`.

### Cards & Sample Showcases
- **Timber & Pattern Plaque:** Completely sharp corners (`0px`), background `#191C1A`, rimmed with an ultra-thin `1px` hairline (`rgba(200, 163, 85, 0.2)`). 
- **Imagery:** High-definition photography with edge-to-edge full bleeds within the card frame. On hover, the image subtly scales (`1.02` scale) within its razor container, accompanied by a transition of the hairline border to 100% `#C8A355`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Minimalist, flat architectural lines. Background is `#141715` with a persistent bottom border of `1px` solid brass, no vertical or top borders.
- **Focus States:** Bottom line thickens to `1.5px` solid `#C8A355` with label animating upward in `label-caps` typography (`#C8A355`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp `16px` squares, no border-radius. Unchecked: `1px` brass hairline border. Checked: Filled with `#C8A355`, displaying an alabaster interior square mark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Sharp diamond motifs (45-degree rotated squares) instead of circular dots to sustain the parquet inlay metaphor.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage Badges:** Sharp rectangular blocks with `4px 10px` padding. Dark green background (`#0C241B`) bounded by a `1px` gold hairline, displaying timber classification, FSC certification, or pattern geometry in `label-caps`.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Marquetry Pattern Comparator:** Dual-screen split viewport with a movable brass caliper line showing Chevron vs. Herringbone grading.
- **Atelier Spec Sheet:** Technical architectural drawer with etched division lines, displaying wood moisture content, wear layer microns, French oak provenance, and custom patination treatments.
