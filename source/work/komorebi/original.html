Okayama Prefectural Tradition

The Triangular Chimney Flue

Modern commercial imitators use hand-held propane weed torches that merely scorch the wood grain. Komorebi preserves the monumental 400-year-old Okayama guild technique: binding three kiln planks into a self-drafting triangular chimney.

Thermal Telemetry

Continuous vortex combustion reaches 1,200°C in under 90 seconds. This extreme thermal shock restructures wood lignin into an elemental carbon crystal shield without damaging core dimensional integrity.

01

Triangular Binding

Three 4-meter air-dried Cryptomeria cedar boards are strapped tightly using wet straw cords, creating a sealed triangular furnace chimney (*Sankaku*).

Flue Draft Induction
02

1,200°C Infernal Draft

Cypress shavings ignite at the bottom. A fierce natural chimney draft pulls oxygen violently upward, consuming cellulose sugars in a roar of controlled flame.

Pyrolytic Carbonization
03

Alpine Spring Quench

At the exact second of optimal alligator cracking, the triangle is kicked open and flash-drowned in mineral mountain spring water, locking the carbon lattice in place.

Thermal Quenching Matrix
04

Botanical Oil Deep Cure

The wood cools slowly before deep impregnation with pure virgin Camellia japonica (Tsubaki) and cold-pressed walnut oil, creating zero-smudge indoor readiness.
