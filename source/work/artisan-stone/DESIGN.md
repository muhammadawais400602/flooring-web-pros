## Brand & Style

### Personality & Vision
Rooted in Mediterranean masonry, centuries of hand-chiseled craft, and sun-drenched architectural purity, this design system balances timeless artisan heritage with contemporary editorial clarity. It evokes quiet luxury, earthy permanence, and organic warmth—recalling weathered limestone courtyards, aged Tuscan villas, and tactile terracotta surfaces baked in afternoon heat.

### Target Audience
Architects, interior designers, conservationists, and discerning homeowners seeking bespoke, authentic surfaces. The user expects an atmosphere of scholarly authority, material tactility, and effortless spatial composure.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Expression
**Mediterranean Classical Editorial**: A fusion of classical architectural balance and understated organic minimalism. Clean, intentional proportioning and ample open space evoke sunlight streaming across dry stone courtyards. Subtle, tactile surfaces replace artificial glossy components, employing dry mineral boundaries, fine stonework rules, and grounded terracotta accents.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from geological strata, sun-baked earth, and Mediterranean flora, preserving natural mineral integrity.

### Role Mapping
- **Primary (`#C2613D` - Terracotta Rust)**: Expresses fire-hardened clay and artisanal energy. Used for primary calls to action, selected active indicators, architectural accent bands, and focal milestones.
- **Secondary (`#5C634B` - Olive Cypress)**: The grounding hue of Mediterranean olive groves and stone garden borders. Applied to secondary tags, specimen origin labels, verified authentications, and complementary badges.
- **Tertiary (`#E6DAC6` - Travertine Beige)**: The textural mid-tone of warm honed sediment. Deployed as surface fills, tonal separators, inactive chip surfaces, and structural panel containers.
- **Neutral (`#2D261E` - Aged Bronze)**: Heavy oxidized iron and deep patinated bronze. Dictates high-contrast typography, crisp line art, structural icon fills, and foundational dividers.
- **Canvas & Surface System**:
  - `surface-base`: `#FBF7EE` (Sun-Bleached Limestone) — primary backdrop.
  - `surface-raised`: `#F5EFE1` (Warm Mortar) — elevated cards, modals, and panel inserts.
  - `border-subtle`: `rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.08)` — delicate joint line resembling chiseled lime mortar.

## Typography

The type system creates deliberate contrast between the classical craftsmanship of stone inscriptions and the utilitarian precision of catalog specifications.

### Editorial Hierarchy
- **EB Garamond (Display & Headings)**: Conveys historical authority, classical architectural balance, and artisanal poise. Used in titles, stone specimen designations, and thematic quotes.
- **Work Sans (Body & Labels)**: Clean, humanist, and highly legible without being industrial. Its open apertures and balanced proportions complement the classic serif, maintaining effortless readability in spec sheets, dimensional lists, and dense navigation.
- **Letter Spacing**: Extended tracking is applied to uppercase labels (`label-md` and `label-sm`) to emulate engraved lapidary stonework. Headings maintain natural or slightly snug tracking to feel cohesive and lyrical.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The spatial rhythm relies on a 12-column architectural grid inspired by classical masonry proportions. Spacious layouts allow stone photography and material tactile swatches to breathe like works of art in an open galleria.

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12 columns, 48px canvas margins, 24px gutters. Max container width capped at 1440px to preserve comfortable reading lengths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters. Multi-column stone galleries collapse to 2-column arrays.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4 columns, 20px canvas margins, 16px gutters. Structural forms stack into vertical architectural registers.

### Spatial Rhythm
Vertical rhythm operates on an 8px modular baseline. Generous block margins (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) separate curated quarry collections, reinforcing quiet prestige and unhurried browsing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial digital drop shadows in favor of natural Mediterranean lighting, relying on soft, diffused ambient light and warm architectural mortar lines.

### Depth Techniques
- **Dry-Joint Outlines**: Flat elements sit flush against the canvas, separated by fine 1px mortar-toned borders (`rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.08)` or `#E6DAC6`).
- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved through stacked material tones: `#FBF7EE` (sun-bleached limestone) hosts `#F5EFE1` (warm mortar) elevated containers.
- **Sunlight Shadows**: When floating elements (modals, active specimen flyouts, dropdowns) require separation, use warm, sun-cast ambient illumination:
  - `elevation-low`: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.03)`
  - `elevation-high`: `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.08), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The geometry reflects softly dressed, hand-honed architectural stone. Edges are never razor-sharp (which feels mass-produced and industrial), nor heavily rounded (which undermines structural weight).

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Buttons, input fields, badges, and chips take on a soft, hand-dressed masonry edge.
- **Large Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Swatch tiles, specimen preview panels, and cards emulate cut-and-tumbled flagstone paver geometry.
- **Special Geometry**: Circular forms are reserved exclusively for circular masonry stamps, material provenance seals, and status pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta Rust (`#C2613D`) background, `#FBF7EE` text, 0.25rem corner radius. Hover shifts to deep kiln terracotta (`#B85433`). Focus ring is a 2px offset border in `#C2613D`.
- **Secondary**: Transparent background, 1px solid border in `#2D261E` (30% opacity), Aged Bronze (`#2D261E`) text. Hover introduces a warm travertine wash (`#E6DAC6` at 35% opacity).
- **Text Link**: Aged bronze text with a terracotta underline offset by 4px, transitioning to solid terracotta on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default**: Honed travertine tint (`#E6DAC6` at 40% opacity) with a subtle border in `rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.1)`. Typography is `label-md` in uppercase.
- **Selected**: Solid Olive Cypress (`#5C634B`) with `#FBF7EE` text, or Terracotta Rust (`#C2613D`) for primary filter criteria.

### Lists & Tables
- **Spec Sheet Lists**: Divided by crisp 1px borders in `rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.08)`. Alternating row highlights are omitted in favor of breathable vertical padding (`space-md`). Key data points use uppercase `label-md` for headers and `body-md` for values.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns**: Background in `#F5EFE1`, bottom-bordered or softly framed with a 1px border (`rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.15)`). Text in `#2D261E`. Placeholder rendered in muted umber (`rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.45)`). Focused state deepens the perimeter line to `#C2613D` with zero blur glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Squared or circular borders in `#2D261E` (40% opacity). Checked state fills with `#C2613D` featuring an authentic natural linen check/dot mark.

### Specimen & Product Cards
- **Structure**: Background in `#F5EFE1` framed by a 1px mortar line. Stone photography sits flush with a hairline border separating imagery from details.
- **Content Hierarchy**: Quarry Origin & Finish set in `label-sm` (Olive Cypress), Product Name in `headline-sm` (EB Garamond, Aged Bronze), and dimensional tolerances in `body-sm`.

### Atelier Custom Components
- **Material Provenance Seal**: A circular medallion containing an architectural seal with `EB Garamond` serif monogram and peripheral tracking denoting authentic Italian/French quarry extraction.
- **Surface Texture Switcher**: An interactive segmented bar toggle (e.g., *Honed*, *Bush-Hammered*, *Tumbled*, *Chiseled*) framed in travertine tones with subtle terracotta indicator highlights.
