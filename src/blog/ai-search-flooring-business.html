"Who's the best hardwood refinisher near me?" More homeowners now type questions like that into ChatGPT, Google's AI answers, Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity instead of scrolling through a page of links. Instead of ten blue links, they get one written answer that names a few businesses.

If your flooring company isn't one of the names, you're invisible in that conversation. Here's how AI search works and what you can do about it.

What "AI search" actually means

AI search tools read information from across the web, then write a summary answer. When someone asks for a local service, the answer is usually built from:

  • Business listings, especially Google Business Profile, plus Bing, Apple Maps, Yelp and similar directories.
  • Reviews: how many, how recent, and what customers say in them.
  • Your website: what services you describe, where you say you work, and how clearly it's written.
  • Mentions elsewhere: local news, supplier and manufacturer directories, trade associations and community sites.

In other words, AI search rewards the same thing good local SEO does: a clear, consistent, well-reviewed presence. It just raises the bar on clarity, because the AI has to be confident enough to name you.

Step 1: Make your website easy for AI to understand

AI tools are good at reading plain language and bad at guessing. Help them:

  • Say exactly what you do and where, in normal sentences: "We install and refinish hardwood floors in Hartford, West Hartford and Glastonbury." Don't make visitors (or AI) piece it together from a logo and a slideshow.
  • Give every service its own page explaining the process, the materials you work with, typical timelines and who it's for.
  • Add a genuine FAQ answering the questions customers really ask: "How long does refinishing take?", "Can you install over tile?", "Do you move furniture?" These match the questions people type into AI tools.
  • Add structured data (schema markup), a behind-the-scenes code layer that labels your business name, services, service area and reviews in a format machines read reliably.

Step 2: Be consistent everywhere

If your website says one phone number, Google says another and Yelp still shows your old address, AI tools see conflicting information and become less likely to recommend you. Check that your business name, address, phone, website and services match on every listing.

Step 3: Earn detailed reviews

AI answers often summarise what reviewers say: "customers praise their dust-free sanding and on-time crews." That summary can only be as good as your reviews. Ask customers to mention what you did and how it went. Specific reviews ("They installed LVP in our basement in two days and it looks amazing") give AI far more to work with than "Great job!"

Step 4: Get mentioned beyond your own website

AI tools trust businesses that other sources talk about. Practical ways for a flooring company to earn mentions:

  • Get listed in your suppliers' and manufacturers' "find an installer" directories.
  • Join your local chamber of commerce and any trade association you qualify for.
  • Sponsor a local team or event that lists its sponsors online.
  • Partner with local builders, designers and realtors who can link to you.

Step 5: Answer instantly when they click

Getting recommended is only half the job. When a homeowner lands on your site, they expect a fast answer. An AI chat assistant on your website can answer common questions at 10pm, collect project details and pass the lead to you, so the interest an AI answer created doesn't go cold overnight.

What not to do

  • Don't publish pages of AI-generated filler text. It doesn't help customers, and search engines and AI tools are increasingly good at ignoring it.
  • Don't list services or areas you don't really cover just to appear in more answers. Bad-fit leads and unhappy reviews follow.
  • Don't expect overnight results. AI tools refresh their information at different speeds, and nobody can guarantee a mention.

The short version

AI search isn't a separate world you need a separate strategy for. It rewards flooring businesses that are clear about what they do, consistent everywhere they're listed, well reviewed, and talked about locally. Do those things well and you'll show up better on Google, on the map and in AI answers at the same time.
