A flooring contractor's website isn't a brochure. It's supposed to bring in estimate requests. Yet a lot of contractor sites sit there like a billboard on a dirt road. Owners pay someone every month, get few calls and blame "SEO." The problem is usually simpler, and it's sitting right on the page.

Here are the seven most common leaks and how to fix each one.

1. Your site is slow on mobile

Most homeowners look for flooring contractors on their phones. If your site takes a long time to load, many of them hit the back button and pick the next result before they ever see your work.

The fix: compress every image and use modern formats like WebP, use good hosting, and remove heavy plugins and sliders you don't need. Test your site on PageSpeed Insights and aim for 90 or higher on mobile. Every site we build includes a 90+ Google PageSpeed score on desktop and mobile.

2. There's no click-to-call button on mobile

Plenty of homeowners would rather tap and call than type. If your phone number isn't a tappable link, ideally in a header that stays visible as they scroll, you're making them work to reach you. Some won't bother.

3. Your contact form asks for too much

Every extra field is another reason to give up. A flooring estimate form only needs the basics: name, phone or email, the type of floor and the job's ZIP code or town. Everything else can happen on the call. Every site we build includes an online estimate request form, and new leads are sent to you instantly by text and email.

4. You don't have pages for the areas you serve

A homeowner in Frisco searching "hardwood installer Frisco" is less likely to call from a page that only talks about Dallas, and Google is less likely to show that page for a Frisco search. Add pages for your main service areas, with real local content: neighborhoods you work in, photos and jobs from that town. Our city-by-city local SEO guide explains how to do it without thin, copy-pasted pages.

5. Your project gallery is missing (or weak)

Flooring is visual. If a homeowner can't see plenty of examples of your work before contacting you, they'll contact someone who shows theirs. Organize photos by floor type, use before/after shots, and keep adding new jobs. Drag-to-compare before/after sliders work especially well for refinishing. See our project galleries.

6. Your Google Business Profile doesn't match your website

Your Google Business Profile is how many homeowners find you in the first place. If it's unclaimed, out of date or lists a different phone number than your site, you lose trust with homeowners and with Google. Claim and verify it, keep your name, address and phone identical everywhere, and link your profile to your website. Our Map Pack guide covers the full setup.

7. You never answer "what will this cost?"

Homeowners want a rough idea before they call. You don't have to publish fixed prices. A line like "most hardwood installs in our area land between [your low price] and [your high price] per square foot, installed" or a simple guide to what affects the price is often enough to earn the call. Saying nothing at all can push people toward competitors who are more open.

What a lead-generating flooring website looks like

  • Loads fast on mobile (90+ on PageSpeed Insights)
  • Click-to-call button that's always easy to reach on phones
  • A short estimate form on every service page
  • Clear pages for each service and your main service areas
  • Plenty of real before/after photos, organized by floor type
  • A Google Business Profile with the same name, address and phone as the site
  • Some honest guidance on pricing
  • A mobile-first layout, not a desktop design squeezed onto a phone
  • Structured data (schema) for your business, services and FAQs
  • Instant text and email alerts when a lead comes in
