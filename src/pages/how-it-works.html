Process questions

Good Questions

More answers on the FAQ page.

What if I don't like the mockup?

Tell us what to change and we'll revise it. If it's still not right, walk away — you haven't paid anything.

What if I'm slow sending photos?

No problem. The 7-day clock starts once we have your photos and info. We can also launch with quality stock photos and swap yours in later.

Do I need to write anything?

No. We write every page. You just review and approve.

What happens to my current website?

We replace it and set up redirects so you keep the Google rankings you've already earned.

How do I ask for changes after launch?

Text or email us. Small edits are included in every monthly plan.
