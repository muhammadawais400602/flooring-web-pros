Pricing questions

The Fine Print, in Plain English

More answers on the FAQ page.

Do I pay anything for the mockup?

No. We design your homepage first, free. You only pay the $300 build fee if you love it and want to go ahead.

Are there setup fees or hidden costs?

No. The $300 build and your monthly plan are the whole price. If you ever ask for something outside your plan, we quote it first.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, SSL, daily backups, security updates and small edits. On Growth it also covers local SEO, Google Business Profile work, review requests and a monthly ranking report.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move up or down any month. Your site stays the same — only the services around it change.

Is there a contract?

No. Cancel the monthly plan anytime. Your domain and content are yours.

Is Google Ads spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid to Google directly from your own account, so you always see exactly where it goes. We build and manage the campaigns on the Dominate plan.
