Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flooring WebPros ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit flooringwebpros.com, contact us, or use our services. If you have any questions, contact us at info@flooringwebpros.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in a form on our website, we collect your name, business name, phone number, email address, city or service area, the type of flooring work you do, and your current website address if you share it. When you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive whatever you choose to send, along with your phone number or email address.

Information from clients. When you become a client, we also collect the business information and materials needed to deliver our services, such as your logo, photos, service list, service areas, and access to accounts you ask us to manage (for example, your Google Business Profile, Facebook or Instagram), and billing details.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time, to deliver and secure the website.

Cookies and tracking

Our website does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tracking. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and send you the confirmation email you request through our forms.
  • To provide, manage and improve our services for clients, including building and hosting websites and managing listings and social media accounts on your behalf.
  • To send invoices, service updates and other messages related to your account.
  • To keep our website and services secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Website hosting (Vercel) to host and deliver our website and forms.
  • Email (Hostinger) to send and receive email, including form notifications and replies.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) when you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • Google for fonts on our website, and for Google Business Profile services we manage for clients.
  • Payment and accounting providers to process payments and keep financial records.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

Websites we build for clients

When we build and host a website for a client, the client decides what information their website collects from their own customers (for example, through an estimate request form or AI chat feature). We process that information on the client's behalf to deliver it to them. Each client is responsible for their own website's privacy notice, and their customers should contact them directly with privacy questions.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client information for as long as you are a client plus the period needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes. When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our website and limiting access to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@flooringwebpros.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
