THE TIMELESS TECHNIQUE

The Four Alchemical Rites of Maâlem Craft

No automated rollers. No synthetic glazes. Each tile is born from the gray sedimentary clay beds of Fès, shaped by human muscle, and fired in wood kilns using olive-crush pomace.

01
Mineral Extraction & Kneading

Fès Marl Clay Sun-Drying

Grey clay soaked in ancient water pits, kneaded by foot to expel air pockets, hand-cast into square biscuits and dried under the scorching sun of the Saïss plain.

sunny Natural 14-Day Solar Cure
02
Pyrolytic Firing

1,000°C Olive-Pit Kilns

Wood kilns fueled by compressed olive residues deliver variable pyrolytic heats. Temperature gradients create organic chromatic variation across the batch.

local_fire_department Zero Fossil Fuel Emissions
03
Natural Vitrification

Raw Mineral Enamel Glass

Silica quartz blended with copper filings (greens), lapis lazuli & cobalt (deep blues), and tin oxides (milky opalescents) poured by hand on the bisque.

palette Pure Oxide Chemistry
04
The Mencharr Chisel

Hairline Beveled Edging

The Master Maâlem sits cross-legged, using a weighted steel chisel to bevel the underside edges of every single tile, enabling tight mortar-less joints.
