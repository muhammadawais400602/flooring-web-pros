Lineage of Maestri Terrazzieri

From 16th-Century Battuto to Modernist Architecture

The Venetian terrazzo floor was conceived not as surface decoration, but as monolithic geology. When Renaissance patricians required floors capable of flexing atop Venice’s shifting wooden pilings, the guild of Maestri Terrazzieri synthesized slaked lime, pozzolanic ash, and discarded Carrara and Verona stone chippings.

Today, Atelier Veneziano honors this uninterrupted lineage. We formulate our mineral binders with genuine hydraulic lime and pure stone powders, polishing each continuous pour by hand through progressive diamond diamond-matrices to reveal an unrepeatable tapestry of geological time.
