The 4-Stage Sukiya Craftsmanship Journey

From the mineral-rich delta marshes of Kyushu to hand-stitched installations in modern private sanctuaries, every floor mat undergoes four seasons of unhurried artisanship.

1
Summer Solstice Harvest

Dew-Season Rush Harvest

Hand-harvested in Yatsushiro, Kumamoto before dawn while morning dew remains. Reeds are measured for core density and graded by botanical length to guarantee unblemished stems.

location_on Yatsushiro Basin, Kumamoto
2
Mud Cleansing & Air Cure

Natural Clay & Sun-Drying

Immersed in mineral-rich Awaji volcanic clay (*Sendo*) to lock in the grass's green chlorophyll and distinctive grassy perfume. Zero industrial dyes, bleaches, or synthetic fire-retardants.

wb_sunny Unheated Solarium Chambers
3
Kyoto Atelier Weaving

Precision Loom & Waradoko

Woven with hemp warp strands using heritage wooden looms. Mats are stretched over natural uncompressed seven-layer straw beds and stitched with tension calibrated to ambient humidity.

4
Miyadaiku Joinery

Nail-Less Installation

Installed on-site by certified temple carpenters (*Miyadaiku*). Mats fit with millimeter friction tolerance (*Kigumi* joints), preventing seasonal buckling and floorboard squeaks.
